About Jengo

Built in Kampala, working with teams everywhere

We help growing businesses in Uganda and around the world put AI and automation to work: carefully engineered, clearly documented and fully owned by our clients.

Our story

Why we started Jengo

Every growing business hits the same wall. The team is capable, but more and more of their day goes to copying data between tools, chasing updates and answering the same questions again and again.

AI and no-code platforms have made it possible to automate much of that work, but only if someone designs it properly. Too many automations are fragile, undocumented and understood by just one person.

Jengo exists to do this properly. We treat automation as engineering: scoped, tested, monitored and handed over so your team can rely on it. We work from Kampala, one of Africa's fastest-growing tech hubs. We serve businesses across Uganda and East Africa in person, and teams in North America, Europe and Australia remotely.
