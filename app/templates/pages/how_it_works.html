How the score works

Each answer is mapped to points in Make, and the points are added together for a score out of 100. The weighting reflects which leads we're best placed to help. Every client's model is tuned to their own sales data.

Example lead-scoring model
SignalWhat it tells usWeight
Service typeHow well the need fits what we do bestup to 35
Budget rangeWhether the scope is realistic for the budgetup to 40
TimelineHow urgent the project isup to 25
Maximum score100
